# Properties Panes

The bottom right pane of the VCell document window contains an **Object Properties** tab that
displays properties of any entity (e.g. species, reaction, structure) selected in the main View Pane
of the document window.

Adjacent tabs provide more information about selected entities, the state of your biomodel, or other
biomodels. Some of these panels may be hidden or visible at times, depending on their relevance to
the selected entity.

![](../../images/PropetriesAndInfoTabsSet.png)
